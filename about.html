OUR STORY

A more thoughtful way
to move forward.

Narnika Industries Pvt. Ltd. is based in Hyderabad, India. We work with bagasse and paper products, with paper customization for hotels, restaurants, and cafés at the heart of what we do today.

EVERYDAY OBJECTS. A DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVE.
WHY NARNIKA

We see possibility
in the everyday.

The materials we choose tell a story about the future we want to create.

Our range brings together bagasse products and paper products in both standard and customized formats. For HoReCa businesses—hotels, restaurants, and cafés—paper customization is our current specialty.

We’re building on that foundation as we expand into retail, medical, and other packaging applications. Each new requirement starts with understanding the product, the brand, and the job the packaging needs to do.
