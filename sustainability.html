OUR APPROACH

Good intentions.
Better questions.

Sustainability is about more than a label. It means looking carefully at what a product is made of, how it is used, and what happens next.

A CONSIDERED PERSPECTIVE

Choose with care.
Use with purpose.
Think beyond.

Our ambition is to make thoughtful alternatives part of everyday life. Clear information is an essential part of that journey.

01 / MATERIALS

Understand what goes in.

For bagasse and paper products, understand the fiber composition and any coatings, linings, inks, or other components. Custom printing is part of that assessment too.

02 / APPLICATION

Find the right fit.

Match the product to its intended use. Temperature, moisture, durability, and food-contact requirements can affect suitability.

03 / AFTER USE

Consider what comes next.

Disposal options depend on the specific material and local facilities. Confirm end-of-life guidance for the product and destination.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Questions
worth asking.

Are all products biodegradable or compostable?

These terms are product-specific. Do not assume that every eco-friendly alternative is compostable or biodegradable. Request the relevant specification, test documentation, and disposal conditions for the selected item.

Are all paper products plastic-free?

Not necessarily. Paper products may use coatings or linings to meet their intended function. For plain and customized products, ask about the paper, coatings, printing, and disposal guidance for the selected format.

How can I evaluate a product for my business?

Start with its purpose, material composition, required performance, and disposal route. Ask about samples and relevant documentation before committing to a product or making environmental claims.

What certifications are available?

Certification needs depend on the product and market. Include any required standards in your enquiry so that the relevant documentation and its scope can be discussed. This website does not claim blanket certification across the range.
