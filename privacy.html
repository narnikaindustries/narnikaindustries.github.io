An overview of how this static website works.

Enquiry details

The enquiry form prepares a draft in your email app or a downloadable text file on your device. The website itself does not send your name, email, or requirements to Narnika or a form-processing service. If you send the email, the details you include are shared with sales@ekomorph.com and processed by the email providers involved. Narnika uses enquiries to respond to your request; you can contact the same address about your information. The website does not save form entries to cookies or browser storage.

Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not add analytics tools, advertising trackers, or non-essential cookies. Images, styles, and scripts used by the redesigned pages are served with the website.

External links

Other websites linked from this site have their own policies. If you choose to share your downloaded enquiry through another service, that service handles the transmission.
